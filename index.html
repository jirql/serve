01 / Waste does not disappear

Out of sight is not out of nature.

A wrapper dropped beside a trail can be carried by wind or rain into streams and forest floor. A clean visit is measured not by what we leave behind, but by what we take home.

A simple forest rule

Pack it in. Pack it out.

Bring a small reusable bag for wrappers, bottles, and food scraps. Sort and dispose of them properly after leaving the forest — never leave waste beside a bin that is already full.

On the trail

Small litter, lasting impact

Plastic fragments can persist and spread. Wildlife may become entangled in waste or mistake it for food.

At the campsite

Food scraps belong in a bin

Leftovers can attract animals and change their feeding habits. Follow local guidance and use secure waste containers.

02 / Forest litter

A forest floor is a living place.

What we leave behind does not stay in one place. Litter can contaminate soil and water, harm animals, and spoil the quiet places people come to enjoy.

  • 01Carry every wrapper, bottle, tissue, and food scrap back out.
  • 02Stay on marked paths; avoid trampling plants to reach or photograph litter.
  • 03Join organized cleanups and follow local rules for sorting and disposal.
03 / Forests & carbon

Forests store carbon — and can release it.

Trees and forest soils hold carbon as they grow. When forests are cleared, degraded, or burned, some stored carbon returns to the atmosphere. Protecting forests helps keep this balance intact.

Living forest

Absorb & store

Through photosynthesis, trees take in carbon dioxide and store carbon in wood, roots, and forest soils.

Forest under pressure

Disturb & release

Deforestation, degradation, and fire can release carbon stored in trees and soils, while reducing the forest’s ability to absorb more.

04 / Small actions matter

Make your next forest visit lighter.

There is no single fix, but everyday choices add up. Start with one habit and bring it with you.

01

Before you go

Choose reusable bottles and containers. Skip unnecessary single-use packaging.

02

While you are there

Use marked trails, respect wildlife, and keep all waste contained in your bag.

03

When you leave

Take everything out, sort it correctly, and share the pack-it-out habit with others.

Leave the forest as alive as you found it.
